Transferring campaign funds

You can move money from your campaign coffer to another senator's, up to what your coffer holds. Your fundraising goal is judged on your balance at the end of the session, so what you give away counts against you.

  1. Check your balance — the Fundraising number on your senator page. That is the most you can send.
  2. Open the form from the Filing Cabinet (Senators → Transfer campaign funds) and pick the recipient. Senators are listed by district.
  3. Enter the amount and submit. More than your balance is refused in full, with an email saying why.
  4. Watch your email. You get a receipt with your new balance; the recipient gets a notice. The site shows the transfer within about an hour.

Know before you send

  • There is no undo. Only the recipient can send money back; a mistaken transfer can be voided by the instructor.
  • Your goal is judged on your balance at the end of the session, not on what you once raised.
  • Transfers are public: both senators' ledgers, the front page, and the Stats page show them.
